**Full description** (4000 max):

Little Takhti is a free learning app for children in Pakistan from Nursery to Grade 3, and for the parents and teachers who help them.

Every week there are new printable worksheets, quiz games and short parent guides for English, Urdu, Maths, Science, Social Studies, Computer and more. Topics follow the order schools teach them, so your child practises what they are learning in class this week.

What's inside
• Printable A4 worksheets with answer keys, ready to print or share
• Quiz games for every topic, with stars and stickers to win
• Word search, memory match, spelling bee and other learning games
• PowerUps: flashcards, doodle, listen and read
• Short stories to read together, in English and Urdu
• A weekly plan for each subject with goals, activities and a story
• Parent guides that explain each topic in five minutes, with ideas to try at home
• Handwriting practice in English, Urdu and Maths

Made for families
• No ads, ever
• No sign-up and no tracking. Your child's nickname, grade and stars stay on your device
• A parent PIN keeps the Parents area, settings and any link that leaves the app for grown-ups
• Download this week's worksheets once and use them with no internet
• Works on phones and tablets

Little Takhti means "little slate". Little steps, high flights: ننھے قدم، اونچی اڑان
