# My Body Song (Little Takhti), Nursery General Knowledge, topic nursery-gk-t1
# Lyrics draft for Google Flow Music. Facts match the worksheets: the five senses (see with eyes, hear with ears, smell with nose, taste with tongue, touch with hands), counting body parts (2 eyes, 1 nose, 2 ears, 2 hands, 5 fingers on one hand, 1 mouth), keeping clean (wash hands before eating, brush teeth at night, take a bath).
# Nursery level: short lines, lots of repetition, action words so children can point and move.
# SOUND prompt (same as the approved Parts of a Plant song):
# upbeat cheerful kids educational pop, acoustic guitar, ukulele, handclaps, bouncy bass, warm female lead with a light South Asian accent, small children choir on chorus, clear diction, extended song structure, spacious 7-second instrumental tick-tock clock percussion timer breaks before every answer, 120 bpm
# Quiz: after each question there must be a 7-second instrumental tick-tock break with NO singing, then the answer.

[Intro]
Hello friends, it's learning time!
Let's sing about our body!
Ready? Let's go!

[Verse 1]
Two eyes, look, look, look,
I see with my eyes, I can see a book!
Two ears, listen, hear,
I hear with my ears, the birds sing near!

[Verse 2]
One nose, sniff, sniff, sniff,
I smell with my nose, a flower, what a whiff!
My tongue says yum, yum, yum,
I taste with my tongue, a sweet mango, yum!

[Verse 3]
Two hands, clap, clap, clap,
I touch with my hands, soft grass in my lap!
Five fingers on one hand,
Wiggle, wiggle, wiggle, aren't they grand!

[Chorus]
Eyes to see and ears to hear,
Nose to smell, so sweet and clear,
Tongue to taste and hands to touch,
I love my body very much!

[Verse 4]
Wash my hands before I eat,
Count to twenty, nice and neat!
Brush my teeth at night, then bed,
Take a bath from toes to head!

[Chorus]
Eyes to see and ears to hear,
Nose to smell, so sweet and clear,
Tongue to taste and hands to touch,
I love my body very much!

[Bridge]
Touch your nose, touch your ears,
Touch your eyes, now clap and cheer!
Wiggle your fingers, stamp your feet,
Jump up high, and take a seat!

[Quiz intro]
Quiz time! Can you guess? Shout it out loud!

[Question 1]
How many eyes do we have?

[7-second extended instrumental tick-tock timer break, no singing]

[Answer 1]
Two eyes! We have two eyes!

[Question 2]
What do we smell with?

[7-second extended instrumental tick-tock timer break, no singing]

[Answer 2]
Our nose! We smell with our nose!

[Question 3]
How many fingers on one hand?

[7-second extended instrumental tick-tock timer break, no singing]

[Answer 3]
Five fingers! Five fingers on one hand!

[Final chorus]
Eyes to see and ears to hear,
Nose to smell, so sweet and clear,
Tongue to taste and hands to touch,
I love my body very much!

[Outro]
Find more fun worksheets and games, on the website and the app! Bye bye, friends!
