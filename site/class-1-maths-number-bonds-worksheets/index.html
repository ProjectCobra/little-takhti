Tips for parents and teachers

Two smaller numbers join to make a bigger number, like 6 and 4 make 10.

  1. Put 10 chickpeas in a line and slide some to one side: 7 here, 3 there.
  2. Draw the bond: a big circle for the whole, two small circles for the parts.
  3. Ask it both ways: "7 and what make 10?" and "10 is 7 and what?"
Try at home: Show 10 fingers and fold some down. How many are still up? Play "make 10" with playing cards.
