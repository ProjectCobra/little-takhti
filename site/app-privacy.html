Privacy policy for the Little Takhti app

For the Little Takhti app on Android and iPhone. Last updated 23 November 2026.

In short: the app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no sign-in. We do not collect any personal information from children or parents. Everything you add in the app stays on your device.

Who the app is for

Little Takhti is a free learning app with worksheets, quiz games, stories and parent guides for children from Nursery to Grade 3 (about ages 3 to 9), and for their parents and teachers. It is made in Pakistan.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or send us a child's or parent's name, email address, phone number, photo, voice, location, contacts or device identifiers. It does not contain advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social media tools.

Information kept on your device

To make the app work, a few things are saved only on the phone or tablet you use:

  • the class (grade) you choose and a nickname for each child if you add one
  • quiz stars, stickers and the daily streak
  • the 4-digit parent PIN, saved in a scrambled (hashed) form
  • worksheet PDFs you open or save for offline use

We cannot see this information. You can delete saved worksheets in Parents > Saved worksheets, and removing the app deletes everything.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to download worksheet PDFs from our website, littletakhti.com, when you open or save a worksheet. These are plain file downloads: no account, cookie or identifier is sent with them. Like any website, the web host may keep short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service running and safe. We do not use these logs to identify or track anyone.

Links and grown-up areas

The Parents area, settings and any link that leaves the app are protected by the parent PIN. Links outside the app open in your web browser, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children's privacy

We follow the Google Play Families Policy and the principles of children's privacy laws such as COPPA. Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or disclose. If you think a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Our website

This policy covers the app only. The Little Takhti website has its own privacy policy, on the website.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we ever want to collect information, we will ask parents first.
