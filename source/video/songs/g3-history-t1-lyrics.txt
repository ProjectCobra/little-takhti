# Mesopotamia Song (Little Takhti), Grade 3 History, topic g3-history-t1
# Lyrics draft for Google Flow Music. Facts match the worksheets and quiz: "land between the rivers", Tigris and Euphrates, today's Iraq, some of the first cities, cuneiform (wedge-shaped writing pressed with a reed into clay tablets), the wheel, the plough, the ziggurat (a stepped temple tower), same time as the Indus Valley.
# SOUND prompt (same as the approved Parts of a Plant song):
# upbeat cheerful kids educational pop, acoustic guitar, ukulele, handclaps, bouncy bass, warm female lead with a light South Asian accent, small children choir on chorus, clear diction, extended song structure, spacious 7-second instrumental tick-tock clock percussion timer breaks before every answer, 120 bpm
# Intro says "learning time", never "Takhti time" (Flow mispronounces Takhti).
# Say clearly: Mes-o-po-TAY-mia, you-FRAY-teez, KYOO-nee-form, ZIG-oo-rat.

[Intro]
Hello friends, it's learning time!
Let's travel back, long, long ago,
To a land between two rivers. Ready? Let's go!

[Verse 1]
Mesopotamia, say it with me,
It means the land between the rivers, you see!
The Tigris and Euphrates flowing side by side,
In the land we call Iraq today, by the riverside.

[Verse 2]
Farmers grew their wheat beside the water there,
Villages grew bigger, with people everywhere.
They built some of the first cities, it's true,
With bricks made of mud, and big walls too!

[Chorus]
Mesopotamia, long ago,
Between two rivers, watch it grow!
Tigris, Euphrates, side by side,
The first big cities, far and wide!
Writing and wheels, a stepped tower too,
Gifts from the past for me and you!

[Verse 3]
How did they write? Not with pen or ink!
They pressed a reed in soft wet clay, just think!
Little wedge-shaped marks in rows so neat,
Cuneiform writing, on a clay tablet sheet!

[Verse 4]
The wheel went round and helped the carts to roll,
The plough dug the fields, that was its role!
The ziggurat stood tall with steps up high,
A temple tower reaching to the sky!

[Chorus]
Mesopotamia, long ago,
Between two rivers, watch it grow!
Tigris, Euphrates, side by side,
The first big cities, far and wide!
Writing and wheels, a stepped tower too,
Gifts from the past for me and you!

[Bridge]
Far away, at the very same time,
The Indus Valley people built their towns so fine.
Two great rivers here, one river there,
People grew by rivers, everywhere!

[Quiz intro]
Quiz time! Can you guess? Shout it out loud!

[Question 1]
Mesopotamia was between two rivers. The Tigris and the...?

[7-second extended instrumental tick-tock timer break, no singing]

[Answer 1]
The Euphrates! The Tigris and Euphrates!

[Question 2]
What did they write on?

[7-second extended instrumental tick-tock timer break, no singing]

[Answer 2]
Clay tablets! Wedge marks in clay, that's cuneiform!

[Question 3]
Mesopotamia is in which country today?

[7-second extended instrumental tick-tock timer break, no singing]

[Answer 3]
Iraq! The land between the rivers is in Iraq!

[Final chorus]
Mesopotamia, long ago,
Between two rivers, watch it grow!
Tigris, Euphrates, side by side,
The first big cities, far and wide!
Writing and wheels, a stepped tower too,
Gifts from the past for me and you!

[Outro]
Find more fun worksheets and games, on the website and the app! Bye bye, friends!
