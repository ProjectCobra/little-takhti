# LGS Defence, Grade 1, December Assessment 2026: syllabus

Shared by Sumbul on 26 Nov 2026 (scan: `lgs-grade1-december-2026-syllabus.pdf`). Use it for Grade 1 practice papers and December revision. Original exercises only; never copy school sheets.

## English
- GINN Reader: "Digger at School", "A duck is a duck" (school readers: do not copy text; write similar short reading passages with questions)
- Grammar: statements and questions; capital letters; names and initials; verbs
- Phonics: ending m, ending d, ending n, ending t
- Creative writing: unseen (short picture or prompt writing)

## Maths (review all resource pack work)
- Numbers to 20: counting; counting on and counting back; counting by making 10; comparing numbers; comparing and ordering; adding by counting on; adding by making 10; adding by ones; subtracting by counting back; subtracting by ones; subtracting from 10; fact families; picture problems
- Numbers to 100: counting; tens and ones; counting by ones and tens; number patterns; comparing by subtracting; comparing and ordering
- Number spellings 0 to 50
- Times tables of 2 and 3
