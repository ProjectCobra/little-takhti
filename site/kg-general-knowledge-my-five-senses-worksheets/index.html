Tips for parents and teachers

Our five senses: sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch.

  1. Do a blindfold smell test with safe kitchen things.
  2. Listen with eyes closed and name the sounds.
  3. Touch things and say rough, smooth, soft or hard.
Try at home: Go on a senses walk in the park. Taste sweet, sour and salty foods.
