## Science: Chapter 2, Plants and Animals (taught mid November)

Topic: how animals grow, life cycles.

- Human stages: baby, child, adolescent, adult, elderly.
- Grasshopper: egg, nymph, adult (incomplete change, nymph looks like a small adult with no wings).
- Sea turtle: egg, hatchling, juvenile, adult.
- Butterfly: egg, larva (caterpillar), pupa, butterfly.
- Frog compared with butterfly: frog is an amphibian, lays eggs in water, breathes with gills then lungs; butterfly is an insect, lays eggs on leaves, breathes through spiracles; both lay eggs and both go through big changes.

Exercise styles used:
- Colour each stage and write 1 to 4 in a circle to show the order, then write the stage names in order.
- Venn diagram comparing two animals (different in the outer parts, same in the middle).
- Draw each stage in a box and write its name from a word box.
- Draw and label a life cycle as a circle with arrows.
- Short written Q and A in the copy: what is a life cycle, first stage of a sea turtle, what is a nymph, how a nymph differs from an adult, name another animal that hatches from an egg.

Level note: vocabulary is higher than our current Grade 1 Science (nymph, juvenile, hatchling, larva, pupa, amphibian, spiracles). Our existing Life cycles topic (`plus_a.js`) covers butterfly, frog and plant only.

## Geography / Social Studies: Maps (taught mid November)

- Cardinal directions: North, South, East, West on a simple town map with a compass rose. Questions like "What is north of the supermarket?" answered by circling a picture or writing the place.
- Map key: match picture symbols (school, hospital, bakery, playground, lake, park) to their names.
- Places used: school, hospital, supermarket, bakery, airport, house, park, fire station, zoo, restaurant, police station, pet shop.

## Calendar (November)

- Monthly calendar sheet: circle all Saturdays, mark every Monday, count Tuesdays, day after Friday, date of the first Sunday, 7 days in a week, colour weekends and weekdays differently, mark today's date.
