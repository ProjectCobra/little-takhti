What your child will learn

  • Write Urdu letters alif to ttay.
  • Write Urdu letters say to khay.
  • Write Urdu letters daal to rray.
  • Write Urdu letters zay to suaad.
  • Write Urdu letters zuaad to ghain.
  • Write Urdu letters fay to laam.
  • Write Urdu letters meem to do chashmi hay.
  • Write Urdu letters hamza to bari yay.

Tips for parents and teachers

Pencil movements from right to left, the direction Urdu is written.

  1. Show that Urdu starts on the right side of the page.
  2. Trace each line in the air from right to left.
  3. Trace on paper from the green dot to the star.
Try at home: Draw right-to-left lines in a tray of flour. Practise with a finger on a takhti or chalkboard.
