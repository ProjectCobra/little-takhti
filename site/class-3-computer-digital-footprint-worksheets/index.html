Tips for parents and teachers

Our digital footprint and thinking before we post anything online.

  1. Talk about what is OK to share online.
  2. Ask: Would I be happy for everyone to see this?
  3. Check privacy settings together.
Try at home: Make a "think before you post" poster. Look at what is online about your family together.
