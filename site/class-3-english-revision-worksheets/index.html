Tips for parents and teachers

Nouns come in kinds: common, proper, collective and abstract (things we feel or think but cannot touch).

  1. Ask: is it any thing (common) or a special name (proper)?
  2. Groups have their own names: a herd, a flock, a team.
  3. If you cannot touch, see or hear it, it may be an abstract noun: love, fear, honesty.
Try at home: Spot collective nouns at the zoo or market: a bunch of bananas, a crowd of people. Name a feeling for each family member: Dada's patience, Ammi's kindness.
