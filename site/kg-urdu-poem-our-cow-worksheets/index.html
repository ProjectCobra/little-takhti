Tips for parents and teachers

Reading a classic poem by Ismail Merathi and talking about what a cow gives us.

  1. Read the poem aloud and let your child repeat each line.
  2. Talk about milk, yogurt and butter.
  3. Find the rhyming words.
Try at home: Visit a dairy or farm. Make lassi together and talk about where milk comes from.
