What your child will learn

  • Know when the Mughal empire began.
  • Name the first six Mughal emperors in order.
  • Know why Akbar is remembered.
  • Know famous Mughal buildings in Pakistan.
  • Describe a Mughal garden.
  • Know the arts of the Mughal period.
  • Know about life at the Mughal court.
  • Use dates to work out time.
  • Know how the Mughal empire ended.

Tips for parents and teachers

The Mughal empire (1526 to 1857): Babur and the first six emperors, Akbar, Mughal buildings in Lahore such as the Badshahi Mosque, Shalimar Gardens and Lahore Fort, gardens, miniature painting, Persian at court and the end of the empire.

  1. Find Lahore, Agra and Delhi on a map.
  2. Look at photos of the Badshahi Mosque and Shalimar Gardens together.
  3. Make a family timeline and compare it with the Mughal timeline.
Try at home: Visit the Lahore Fort, Badshahi Mosque or Shalimar Gardens, or watch a short video tour. Try drawing a small miniature painting with fine pencils and bright colours.
