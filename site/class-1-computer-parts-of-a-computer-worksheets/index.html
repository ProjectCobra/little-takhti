Tips for parents and teachers

The main parts of a computer and computers we see around us.

  1. Point to each part of a computer or laptop and name it.
  2. Talk about devices at home that have a computer inside.
  3. Let your child press the power button with you.
Try at home: Draw and label a computer. Look for screens and buttons on machines when you go out.
